🙏 Mission Trip Ready

Bulgarian is phonetic — once you know the alphabet, you can read almost everything. Start with the 30 letters, then master the survival phrases. Locals deeply appreciate any effort to speak their language.

Important cultural note: Bulgarians nod their head for “no” and shake it for “yes” — the opposite of most countries. Watch body language carefully!
